## Discrimination

The T3 gate rejects plausible wrong implementations:

- collapsing resources and resource versions into one table;
- storing epistemic evidence in T2 operational artifacts;
- creating claims without `derived_from` provenance;
- mutating resource versions instead of preserving history;
- introducing awareness authority during T3.

The focused fixtures also discriminate against fake richness for empty targets,
`.sidecar` leakage into target observations, receipt/journal side effects during
substrate refresh, and loss of prior evidence after target content changes.

## Remaining risks

The substrate is intentionally thin. It records filesystem resources, byte hashes,
minimal inventory/hash observations, content-addressed JSON evidence, and only simple
derived claims. It does not yet provide awareness revisions, model summaries,
domain-specific cartridges, semantic/vector retrieval, object-store contracts, mutation
preview/apply governance, verification workflow, MCP, release/update proof, or clean
removal proof. Those remain owned by later tranches.
