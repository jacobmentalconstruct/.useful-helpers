## Project closure

Project closure requires all of the following:

1. Product STOP is satisfied under the Product Charter.
2. No known correctness or safety blocker remains.
3. Every active construction tranche has an operator-granted terminal disposition.
   PARKED, SUPERSEDED, and WITHDRAWN are legitimate dispositions; no fictional parking
   is required.
4. Final sealed release evidence and its identity are recorded.
5. A final closure entry is operator approved.

without it.
